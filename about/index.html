The Story Behind M2pdf

Managing critical business communications, like financial invoices, legal correspondence, and sensitive client records, requires absolute privacy.

When we were looking for a secure way to archive document workflows for our firm, 3dfusionstudio, and managing strict corporate compliance with our company secretary, we discovered a massive security flaw in the market. Almost every tool available to convert Gmail emails to PDF operated by uploading the user's inbox data to a remote third-party server.

Uploading confidential corporate data to an unknown server just to change a file format is a compliance nightmare. We refused to take that risk, and we knew other professionals wouldn't want to either.

Privacy By Default

We decided to build a better, safer way. M2pdf was engineered from the ground up as a zero-trust archiving tool.

Instead of relying on external processing, M2pdf leverages the power of your own web browser. Every single byte of data is processed 100% locally on your device. Your emails, attachments, and private records are never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone.

M2pdf is trusted by legal teams, independent accountants, and businesses worldwide who demand frictionless workflows without compromising on data security. If your team uses Gmail and needs a reliable, private way to backup records, this extension was built for you.
