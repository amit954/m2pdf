Gmail Wasn't Built to Hand Your Emails Back

If you've tried to get more than a couple of emails out of Gmail, you've run into these.

One email at a time

Open, Print, Save as PDF, type a file name, close. Then do it again for the next one. Twenty emails is twenty rounds of the same clicks.

Attachments get left behind

Gmail's print view lists attachment names but doesn't include the files. You download each one separately and work out later which email it belonged to.

Online converters want your inbox

Many tools work by connecting to your Gmail account or processing your messages on their servers. That's a lot of access to hand over just to get a PDF.
