How to Save a Gmail as PDF Manually (Free, Built-In Method)

Gmail has a built-in way to save a single email as a PDF using your browser's print dialog. It's free and takes a few clicks. Here's exactly how to do it:

1.

Open the email. In Gmail, open the message (or conversation) you want to save.

2.

Open the print view. Click the three-dot "More" menu at the top-right of the email and choose Print (or press Ctrl + P / Cmd + P).

3.

Choose "Save as PDF". In the print dialog, set the Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF.

4.

Save the file. Click Save, pick a folder, and your email is exported as a PDF.

The catch: this method works for one email at a time, and it does not include your attachments. They're simply left out of the PDF. It also carries over sidebar and header clutter, and can cut off long threads.

Why the Built-In Method Isn't Enough

If you've ever relied on Ctrl+P or Gmail's "Print all" icon for anything important, you already know the frustration. It creates messy documents, includes unnecessary sidebar menus, can't batch multiple emails at once, and worst of all, it completely leaves behind your attachments.
