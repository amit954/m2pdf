Free Convert MBOX to PDF With Attachments

Need to open and convert an MBOX file to PDF? Our free online tool lets you read MBOX mailboxes and save any email as a PDF, right in your browser. MBOX is the mailbox format used by Gmail Takeout, Mozilla Thunderbird, Apple Mail, and other clients. A single MBOX file can hold hundreds or thousands of emails in one archive, along with their attachments, sender details, and formatting.

Using Gmail? Try our M2pdf Chrome Extension to convert Gmail emails to PDF locally in your browser, with attachments embedded.

Open and Convert MBOX to PDF Without Installing Software

Drag and drop your .mbox file onto the converter. The tool reads the mailbox and lists every email inside it. Click any email to view it with its original formatting and attachments, then use the "Print / Save PDF" button to create a clean PDF copy of that message.

The entire process runs inside your web browser. There is nothing to download or install, and your mailbox never leaves your device, which makes it a safe choice for sensitive or confidential archives.

How to Convert an MBOX File to PDF

  1. Drag and drop your .mbox file, or click Browse Files to select it.
  2. Wait a moment while the mailbox is read and its emails are listed.
  3. Click any email in the list to open it with attachments.
  4. Click "Print / Save PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" to download that email.

Where Do MBOX Files Come From?

  • Gmail: Exported through Google Takeout as a .mbox archive.
  • Mozilla Thunderbird: Stored natively in the MBOX format.
  • Apple Mail: Exported as an MBOX mailbox.
  • Other clients: Many email apps import and export MBOX.
